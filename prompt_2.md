# Hero, Statistics & Footer Section Prompt (Reference-Based)

## Objective
Create a **professional, modern, and trust-focused HERO, STATISTICS, and FOOTER section**
inspired by the provided reference website image.

⚠️ Important Rules:
- Do NOT copy any text, images, branding, or code from the reference
- Use original content and placeholders
- Replicate layout structure, UX flow, spacing, and visual hierarchy only

---

## HERO SECTION

### Purpose
- Immediately communicate experience, credibility, and industry expertise
- Drive users toward Services and Contact actions
- Create a strong first impression

---

### Hero Layout
- Full-width hero section (100% viewport width)
- High-quality industrial / manufacturing / HVAC themed background image
- Dark overlay or gradient overlay for text readability
- Content centered vertically and horizontally
- Optional subtle parallax or fixed background effect

---

### Hero Content Structure
- Primary headline (H1):
  Example:
  "More than 20 Years of Innovation & Growth"

- Supporting subheading (1–2 lines):
  Short value proposition highlighting experience, reliability, and quality

- Call-to-Action Buttons:
  - Primary CTA: "Explore Services"
  - Secondary CTA: "Contact Us"

---

### Hero CTA Styling
- Primary button:
  - Solid brand color
  - Rounded corners
  - Hover animation (slight scale or color change)

- Secondary button:
  - Outline style
  - Transparent background
  - Hover fill effect

---

### Hero UX & Accessibility
- Fade-in or slide-up animation for headline and buttons
- Smooth hover transitions
- Mobile responsive layout:
  - Text centered
  - Buttons stacked vertically on smaller screens
- High color contrast
- Keyboard-accessible buttons

---

### Hero Technical Requirements
- Semantic HTML (<section>, <h1>, <p>, <a>)
- Optimized background image
- SEO-friendly heading structure

---

## STATISTICS / ACHIEVEMENTS SECTION

### Purpose
- Build instant credibility and trust
- Highlight company scale, experience, and success
- Provide scannable proof points for users

---

### Statistics Layout
- Full-width or contained section below main content
- Light background with subtle patterns or gradients
- 4-column layout on desktop
- 2-column on tablet, stacked on mobile
- Large numeric values with short labels

---

### Statistics Content Structure
Each stat block should include:
- Large bold number (counter animation)
- Short descriptive label below it

Example placeholders:
- "160+ CR" – Revenue Generated
- "460+" – Clients & Collaborations
- "510+" – Successfully Completed Projects
- "20+" – Years of Experience

---

### Statistics UX & Animation
- Animated counters that increment on scroll
- Smooth ease-in animation
- Animation triggers only once when section enters viewport
- Numbers remain readable without animation fallback

---

### Statistics Styling
- Bold typography for numbers
- Subtle accent color for highlights
- Icons optional (minimal, line-based)
- Clear spacing between stats

---

### Statistics Technical Requirements
- Use JavaScript or CSS for counter animation
- Accessible text (no important info hidden in animation)
- Semantic HTML (<section>, <div>, <h3>, <p>)
- SEO-friendly visible content (not canvas-only)

---

## FOOTER SECTION

### Purpose
- Provide essential contact and navigation links
- Improve SEO and trust
- Encourage conversions

---

### Footer Layout
- Dark-themed footer section
- 4-column layout on desktop
- Stacked layout on mobile
- Consistent spacing and alignment

---

### Footer Column 1 – CTA Section
- Heading:
  "Don’t Wait, Stay Cool With Us"

- Short description encouraging consultation or service inquiry

- CTA Button:
  "Schedule Now"
  - Brand color
  - Rounded corners

---

### Footer Column 2 – Contact Information
- Heading: "Get in Touch"
- Phone number (clickable)
- Email address (clickable)
- Physical office address (multi-line)

---

### Footer Column 3 – Quick Links
- Heading: "Quick Links"
- Links:
  - Government Certificates
  - Financial Statements
  - Accreditations
  - About Us
  - Contact Us
  - Terms & Conditions
  - Privacy Policy

---

### Footer Column 4 – Branding & Social
- Company logo
- Short tagline or brand statement
- Social media icons:
  - Facebook
  - Twitter
  - LinkedIn
  - Instagram

---

### Footer Bottom Bar
- Thin divider line
- Copyright text:
  © [Current Year] [Company Name]. All Rights Reserved.

---

## Footer UX & Styling
- Hover states on links and icons
- Balanced spacing
- Clear visual hierarchy
- Semantic <footer> tag

---

## SEO & Accessibility Requirements
- Proper heading hierarchy (H1–H4)
- Descriptive internal links
- ARIA labels for icons if needed
- Alt text for logos
- Fast loading, optimized assets

- Dark theme footer
